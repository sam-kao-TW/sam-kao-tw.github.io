關於About

我的研究位於人工智慧、資訊安全經濟學與管理理論的交會處，以資源基礎觀點（RBV）與制度理論，探討組織如何導入與治理 AI 驅動的資安能力。博士論文聚焦於 AI 驅動之內部威脅偵測。

在學術研究之外，我在公部門累積近十年的資訊與資安實務經驗，並於 2017–2024 年間在明新科技大學資訊工程系擔任兼任講師，講授程式設計、伺服器管理與資訊安全課程。

指導教授：皮世明 教授

My research sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, cybersecurity economics, and management theory. Drawing on the Resource-Based View and Institutional Theory, I study how organizations adopt and govern AI-driven security capabilities. My dissertation focuses on AI-driven insider threat detection.

Alongside research, I bring nearly ten years of hands-on IT and cybersecurity experience in the public sector, and taught programming, server administration, and information security as an adjunct lecturer at Minghsin University of Science and Technology from 2017 to 2024.

Advisor: Prof. Shih-Ming Pi
